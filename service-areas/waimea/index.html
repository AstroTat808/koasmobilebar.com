How it works

A practical bar plan for events in North Hawaiʻi.

Waimea celebrations often have their own venue and access considerations. We use the address, timing, guest count and desired service style to plan a bar setup that fits the event rather than forcing the event into a preset format.

Mobile bar option

Bring a dedicated bar setup to venues or private-event locations when logistics allow.

Bartender-only option

Use professional bartenders when the location already has an appropriate bar.

Travel planned up front

Your exact event location is used to determine transportation and setup requirements.

Any celebration style

Birthdays, graduations, company events, weddings and private parties can all be quoted.
