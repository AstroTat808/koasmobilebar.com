Questions, answered

Know what is included before you book.

Does Koa's Mobile Bar provide the alcohol?

No. Koa's is a dry-bar service. You purchase the alcohol and we help build a shopping list around your guest count, package and drink menu.

What is the difference between the three packages?

Big Island ($2,500) is the premium, widest-menu experience with beer, champagne, wine, two signature drinks and mixed cocktails. Maui ($2,000) adds two signature drinks to beer, champagne and wine for a more personalized menu without full mixed-cocktail service. Oahu ($1,500) keeps service streamlined around beer, champagne and wine.

How many guests and service hours are included?

The standard service baseline covers up to 100 guests and four hours of service. Additional guests are $8 each and additional service hours are $200 per hour.

How is bartender staffing priced?

Bartender labor is $65 per hour per bartender. Most events use one or two bartenders depending on guest count, menu complexity and service flow.

How does travel pricing work?

There is no travel fee within 20 miles. Travel beyond the first 20 miles is $1.50 per mile.

What are the bartender gratuity options?

You can choose a 10% fee per bartender with a tip jar present or a 25% fee per bartender with no tip jar.

What supplies are included?

The service foundation includes a water station, comprehensive bar supplies, up to 150 lbs of ice, chilling buckets, fresh garnishes, napkins, biodegradable cups, five selected mixers, available generator hookup, general liability insurance and a planning consultation.

Do you only serve weddings?

No. Koa's Mobile Bar serves birthdays, graduations, corporate events, private parties, showers, anniversaries, holiday events, weddings and other celebrations across Hawaiʻi Island.
