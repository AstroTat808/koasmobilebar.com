Questions, answered

Before you book.

Do you only serve weddings?

No. Koa's Mobile Bar is designed for birthdays, graduations, corporate events, private parties, showers, anniversaries, holiday events, weddings and other celebrations across Hawaiʻi Island.

Is the trailer required?

Tell us what your venue and event need. We can quote the appropriate bar setup and staffing based on your location, event format and service plan.

What affects the final price?

Event location, travel, guest count, service duration, staffing, package selection and optional enhancements can affect the final quote.

How far in advance should I inquire?

Earlier is better for popular dates. Submit your date as soon as you know it and we'll confirm availability.

Do you travel around the island?

Yes. Koa's Mobile Bar is built for events across Hawaiʻi Island. Travel and mileage are reflected in the final quote when applicable.
