Bartender-only or full setup

Some weddings need the Koa's trailer. Others already have a built-in bar, rental bar or suitable beverage station. The useful question is not whether you need more equipment—it is what your venue, menu and guest count actually require for efficient service.

Venue coordination

Wedding flow

Plan for the rushes, not only the average guest count.

Wedding bars experience predictable demand spikes: immediately after the ceremony, when cocktail hour opens, after dinner and when dancing starts. A simple menu with the right staffing can often outperform a complicated menu with too many made-to-order choices.

For that reason, Koa's looks at the number of signature drinks, beer-and-wine demand, service window, guest arrival pattern and whether the bar is serving during transitions. Those details matter more than choosing a bartender count from a generic rule of thumb.

Frequently asked

Big Island wedding bar planning.

Can we hire Koa's bartenders without the mobile bar trailer?

Yes. If your wedding venue already has a suitable bar or service area, Koa's can quote bartender-only service.

Who purchases the alcohol?

Koa's uses a dry-bar model. The client purchases the alcohol and Koa's plans the agreed service, staffing and included supplies.

How many bartenders do we need?

Staffing is based on guest count, beverage menu, service duration and expected demand. The final quote confirms the bartender count for the specific wedding.

Can Koa's work with our planner or venue coordinator?

Yes. Bar placement, setup timing, service flow, venue requirements and breakdown can be coordinated with the planner or venue team.
