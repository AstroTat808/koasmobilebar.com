Frequently asked

Planning questions.

Can you serve company parties and client events?

Yes. Koa's Mobile Bar can be quoted for company celebrations, hosted receptions, client events and similar corporate gatherings.

Can you provide bartender-only service?

Yes. If the venue already has an appropriate bar or service area, bartender service can be quoted separately from the mobile bar setup.

Can you coordinate with our planner or venue contact?

Yes. Provide the relevant venue and event contacts during planning so logistics can be aligned before event day.
