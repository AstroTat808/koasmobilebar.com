Frequently asked

Planning questions.

Can you serve milestone birthdays?

Yes. Koa's Mobile Bar can be quoted for milestone birthdays and other private birthday celebrations across Hawaiʻi Island.

Can you work at a private home?

Potentially, yes. Share the address or area, guest count and access details so the setup and travel can be evaluated.

Do you provide the alcohol?

Koa's operates as a dry-bar service: the client purchases the alcohol and the quote defines the bar service, staffing and included supplies.
