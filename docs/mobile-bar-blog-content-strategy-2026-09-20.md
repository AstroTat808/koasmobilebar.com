# Koa's Mobile Bar Blog / Content Strategy — 2026-09-20

## Role of the blog

The blog exists to answer high-intent questions that sit between a search and a quote. It should not become a generic cocktail-recipe publication.

Primary conversion paths:
- Article -> wedding-bartenders
- Article -> bartender-service
- Article -> weddings
- Article -> service-area page
- Article -> quote form

## Cannibalization rules

- koasmobilebar.com owns island-wide mobile bar, bartender service, wedding bartender and geographic bar-service intent.
- koasevents.com owns venue, wedding-venue, private-event venue and Koa's Events wedding-package intent.
- Do not duplicate the existing Koa's Events article "Koa’s Mobile Bar FAQ: Wedding & Event Bar Service on Hawaiʻi Island."
- Where an existing Koa's Events article is useful, link to it rather than reproducing it.

## Editorial standard

Every article should:
- Answer one concrete planning question.
- Include Koa's actual service model where relevant.
- Distinguish bartender-only and mobile-bar service.
- Use examples rather than invented client stories.
- Link to one primary conversion page and 2–4 supporting pages.
- Avoid unsupported legal advice.
- Use Article + Breadcrumb structured data.
- Use a real Koa's image, never stock imagery.
- Include a visible updated/published date.
- Be materially different from the corresponding service page.
