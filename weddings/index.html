How it works

Beautiful enough for the photos. Practical enough for service.

The bar has to do two jobs at a wedding: serve guests efficiently and fit the visual language of the event. We plan around your venue, guest count, menu and service timing so the bar supports both.

Frequently asked

Planning questions.

Do you provide the alcohol for weddings?

No. Koa's Mobile Bar uses a dry-bar model, so the client purchases the alcohol.

Can you serve a welcome party or rehearsal dinner?

Yes. Those events can be quoted separately based on location, timing, guest count and service needs.

Can you work at venues outside Koa's Events?

Yes. Koa's Mobile Bar is intended to travel to events around Hawaiʻi Island, subject to location and setup logistics.
